Rebates in 2026 are a maze

Programs keep closing

The federal Greener Homes Grant and Loan are closed. Oil-to-heat-pump advance funding in NB closed to new registrations on June 30, 2026. A new federal heat pump rebate was announced in October 2026 but isn't open yet.

The free program is full

NB Power's Enhanced Energy Savings Program (household income $70,000 or less) is fully subscribed, with a waitlist that could reach two years.

One wrong step costs you

Buy before your energy evaluation, miss the 9-month window, or keep the wrong kind of receipt, and the rebate can be refused.

Questions

Is this an official energy evaluation?

No. Only an NRCan Registered Energy Advisor working with a Save Energy NB service organization can do the official Home Energy Evaluation ($99 + HST through the Total Home program). Our plan helps you understand your options and get ready for that evaluation, and it helps you act on the advisor's report afterwards.

Who's behind this?

Brown Verno, a New Brunswick renovator (drywall, doors, windows, trim, flooring, painting). Plans are drafted with AI tools and reviewed by a person before they're sent.

Do you guarantee I'll get a rebate?

No. Programs are run by NB Power and the Government of Canada, and they decide eligibility. We point you to the official rules and help you avoid common mistakes.

What do you do with my photos and bills?

We use them only to prepare your plan, don't sell them, and delete them on request.
